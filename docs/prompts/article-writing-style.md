# Article Writing Style Prompt Source

Purpose: central writing style guidance for AImagician article generation.

## Baseline

- Write dense, useful, evidence-aware articles.
- Prefer concrete mechanisms, trade-offs, failure modes, and engineering implications over generic explanation.
- Keep the opening hook human and scene-based, but do not label it as "真实场景钩子".
- Use clear section titles that match the article style. Humorous pieces can use sharper titles; interview series should be precise and systematic.
- Do not turn "标题", "摘要", or "目录预览" into reader-visible body sections.

## Structure

- Start with the opening hook.
- Then include a short quote/golden-line block in platform formatters.
- Then include a concise table of contents.
- Body sections should develop the argument and avoid padded repetition.
- Reference/source sections should match the article type and should not fabricate academic references.
