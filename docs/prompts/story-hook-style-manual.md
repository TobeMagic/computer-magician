# Story Hook Style Manual Prompt Source

Purpose: guide opening hooks so articles feel authored rather than templated.

## Hook Principles

- Start from a specific human situation, question, mistake, message, or work scene.
- Do not prefix the text with "真实场景钩子".
- Match the article style:
  - Technical interview: concrete interview failure or design discussion.
  - Product insight: what changed and why it feels like a turning point.
  - Personal growth: first-person reflection and felt experience.
  - Humorous hot topic: a vivid conflict or absurd detail.
- Keep the hook short enough to enter the main argument quickly.
