# Xiaohongshu First-Person Style Prompt Source

Purpose: guide shareable first-person social copy and WeChat newspic drafts.

## Style Rules

- Write from "I just tried/read/saw this" perspective.
- Use short paragraphs and concrete reactions.
- Explain less mechanism; show more scene, emotion, and why someone would share it.
- The first screen matters most: title, first sentence, and cover must carry the click.
- End with a light interaction prompt.

## Avoid

- Third-person product explanation.
- Overly rational product analysis.
- Fake excitement without a concrete scene.
- Long dense paragraphs.
